# Q3 Business Report

## Summary
Revenue for the third quarter was 4.2 million, up 12% on the second quarter and
8% on the same quarter last year. Operating costs rose 5%, mostly hiring.

## Highlights
- Enterprise accounts grew from 41 to 47.
- Churn fell to 2.1% from 2.8%.
- The new onboarding flow cut time-to-first-value from 14 days to 9.

## Risks
- Two large renewals fall in the fourth quarter.
- Hiring for support is behind plan.

## Outlook
We expect fourth-quarter revenue between 4.4 and 4.6 million.
